# Clinical Compliance Guidelines

**REQUIRED READING before posting any mental health content.**

These guidelines are non-negotiable. All Scout-generated content must comply.

---

## Hard Boundaries — Never Cross

### 1. No crisis content

Never depict, describe, or imply:
- Self-harm (cutting, burning, or any physical harm)
- Suicidal ideation (thoughts of ending one's life)
- Suicide attempts
- Acute panic attacks presented as entertainment
- Severe dissociation or psychosis

This applies to hooks, scenes, voiceovers, text overlays, and captions.

### 2. No medical claims

Never state or imply that Aurie:
- Treats, cures, or diagnoses any condition
- Is clinically proven (unless citing a specific published study)
- Is recommended by doctors, therapists, or mental health professionals
- Can replace professional treatment

Permitted: "helps you feel less alone", "a space to process what's happening", "support when you
need it"

### 3. No diagnostic labels as hooks

Never use clinical diagnoses casually:
- Bad: "Are you a narcissist?"
- Bad: "Signs you have BPD"
- Bad: "Is your boss a psychopath?"

These trivialise serious conditions and can mislead users.

---

## Required Inclusions for Heavy Topics

### When content touches on:
- Suicidal thoughts (even obliquely — "I didn't want to be here")
- Self-harm
- Crisis situations
- Severe depression or hopelessness

**Always include** one of these in the caption/CTA:

```
If you're in crisis, text or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
```

```
If you or someone you know is struggling, help is available. Text HOME to 741741.
```

```
In a crisis? Text or call 988, available 24/7.
```

### When content touches on:
- Trauma (general reference — not graphic depictions)
- Grief
- Burnout / exhaustion
- Relationship difficulties

Include a gentle redirect if appropriate:
```
Talk to someone today — Aurie is here, or reach out to a trusted person in your life.
```

---

## Sensitive Topic Handling

| Topic | Approach | Required addition |
|---|---|---|
| Anxiety | Normalise, validate, offer hope | None required |
| Depression | Acknowledge, avoid minimising | "If you're struggling, help is available" |
| Burnout | Validate as real, not weakness | None required |
| Trauma | Reference gently, no graphic details | None if mild; 988 ref if intense |
| Grief | Acknowledge without resolution | None required |
| Relationship difficulties | Validate without assigning blame | None required |
| Self-harm (any reference) | DO NOT INCLUDE — skip this content | Escalate to human review |
| Suicidal ideation | DO NOT INCLUDE — skip this content | Escalate to human review |

## Platform Policies

### TikTok
- Uses AI to detect self-harm / suicide content — will remove or restrict
- Use "unalive" language sparingly and only in educational contexts
- Wellness content should follow the community guidelines for safe messaging

### Instagram / Meta
- "Sensitivity screens" applied to content related to self-harm
- Accounts flagged repeatedly lose Reels distribution
- Follow the WHO safe messaging guidelines on suicide

### General
- Any content that could trigger vulnerable viewers needs a content warning
- Safe messaging guidelines: https://reportingonsuicide.org/recommendations/

---

## Escalation Protocol

If Scout generates content that:
- References self-harm or suicide even obliquely in a way that could be harmful
- Makes medical claims Scout shouldn't make
- Depicts crisis content

**Do not post.** Flag to the human reviewer for assessment before any distribution.

---
