# UGC Hook Formulas

The first 3 seconds determine everything. These are proven hook structures for Aurie content.

---

## Hook Categories

### POV Hooks

Frame the viewer in the scenario. Creates instant identification.

```
POV: you finally asked for help
POV: anxiety brain at 2am won't let you sleep
POV: you realise you've been running on empty for months
POV: you stop pretending you're fine
POV: you find something that actually helps
POV: you tell someone how you're really doing
POV: you take a 5-minute break and it changes your whole day
```

### "Nobody talks about" Hooks

Surfaces a hidden or taboo truth. High shareability.

```
Nobody talks about what anxiety actually feels like
Nobody talks about Sunday night dread
Nobody talks about how exhausting it is to seem fine
Nobody talks about parent burnout
Nobody tells you about the quiet kind of depression
Nobody talks about what burnout looks like when you're still functional
```

### "What if I told you" Hooks

Challenge assumption or offer a surprising reframe.

```
What if I told you 5 minutes a day could change everything
What if I told you your Sunday anxiety has a name
What if I told you feeling this way doesn't mean you're weak
What if I told you rest is part of recovery
What if I told you asking for help is actually the hard part
```

### Direct statement hooks

Short, declarative. Works well with text overlay on still opening shot.

```
This is what anxiety actually looks like
Burnout isn't just being tired
Therapy has a 3-month waiting list. This doesn't.
Your feelings are valid — even the ones you can't explain
It's okay to not be okay — even when everything looks fine
```

### Story opener hooks

First-person narrative that invites the viewer in.

```
I didn't know what was wrong. I just knew something was.
I stopped pretending I was fine. Here's what changed.
I used to think I just needed to push through. I was wrong.
I've been on 3 waiting lists for 6 months. Then I found this.
I finally told someone how I was really doing.
```

## Hook + Visual Pairing Guide

| Hook type | Best visual | Camera |
|---|---|---|
| POV | Close shot of hands, phone, or face | Handheld, slightly shaky |
| Nobody talks about | Wide shot of everyday scene | Static then slow drift |
| What if | Person looking off-screen, then to camera | Slow zoom in |
| Direct statement | Text on dark/blurred background | Static |
| Story opener | Medium shot of person exhaling or settling | Slow push in |

---

## Hook Anti-patterns

Avoid these — they underperform for Aurie's audience:

- **Generic wellness hooks**: "Start your mental health journey today!" — too vague
- **Toxic positivity**: "You've got this!" — dismisses struggle
- **Clinical language**: "Managing your anxiety disorder..." — creates distance
- **Hype/urgency**: "WARNING: This will change your life!" — incongruent with tone
- **Celebrity comparison**: "What [celebrity] does for their mental health" — feels sponsored

## Seasonal / trending hook timing

When a trending topic maps to Aurie's audience, adapt an existing formula:

```
Template: [hook formula] + [trending topic reference] + Aurie angle

Example trend: "quiet quitting"
→ "Nobody talks about what quiet quitting does to your mental health"
→ "POV: you quietly quit — and the anxiety didn't go with the job"

Example trend: "sleep anxiety"
→ "What if I told you racing thoughts at 3am have a fix that isn't melatonin"
→ "Nobody talks about sleep anxiety — it's not just insomnia"
```
