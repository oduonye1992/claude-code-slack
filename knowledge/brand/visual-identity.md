# Aurie Visual Identity

## Colour Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary brand | Soft purple | `#7B68EE` | Overlays, CTA buttons, end cards |
| Secondary brand | Warm lavender | `#9B89F5` | Backgrounds, gradient accents |
| Text / overlay | Warm white | `#FAFAF8` | On-screen text |
| Accent warm | Terracotta | `#D4846A` | Highlights, emotion moments |
| Nature grounding | Sage green | `#8FAF8A` | Nature scenes, calm moments |
| Neutral base | Warm grey | `#F2EFE8` | Background washes |

## Lighting

**Always:**
- Warm colour temperature (3000–4500K equivalent)
- Soft, diffused sources — window light, floor lamps, candles
- Golden-hour or late-afternoon light is ideal
- Slight shadows are fine — adds authenticity

**Never:**
- Harsh studio lighting or ring lights (clinical / influencer feel)
- Cold blue-white fluorescent light
- Flat, evenly lit faces (removes emotion)

## Colour Grading for Video

- Slightly desaturated — take vibrancy down ~10–15% from natural
- Warm lift in shadows — pull shadows toward amber, not blue
- Soft highlight roll-off — no blown-out whites
- Film grain at low level (adds authenticity)
- Luts reference: "warm film", "vintage matte", "soft fade"

## Locations and Settings

**Authentic everyday environments — always:**
- Home: sofa, bed, kitchen table, bathroom mirror
- Transit: train, bus, walking outside
- Coffee shops: worn tables, background noise
- Parks: natural light, movement
- Workspace: desk, laptop, slight chaos

**Never:**
- Pristine, Instagram-perfect interiors
- Obviously fake or stock-looking backdrops
- Clinical settings (unless contrasting with Aurie's warmth)

## Subjects and Representation

- **Age range**: 18–45 primarily; occasional 45–55 (Carla persona range)
- **Diversity**: rotate across skin tones, body types, gender presentations
- **Style**: casual everyday clothes — not styled outfits
- **Hair**: natural, lived-in — not salon-ready
- **Authentic imperfection**: one wrinkle, slightly messy desk, imperfect posture

## Typography (Text Overlays)

- Font style: clean sans-serif (Inter, DM Sans, or equivalent)
- Weight: regular for body, medium for hooks — never bold-screaming
- Colour: warm white `#FAFAF8` on dark, or soft purple `#7B68EE` on light
- No drop shadows — subtle backdrop blur if needed for legibility
- Letter spacing: slightly open (0.02em) for warmth
