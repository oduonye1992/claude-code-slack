# Aurie Brand Voice

## Personality

Aurie sounds like your most emotionally intelligent friend — the one who actually gets it without
making you explain yourself. Warm. Direct. Never preachy.

## Core Tones

| Tone | What it sounds like | What it avoids |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | "That sounds really hard" | "I understand your feelings" (robotic) |
| Empathetic | "A lot of people feel this way" | "You're not alone!" (hollow) |
| Conversational | "What's been on your mind?" | "Please describe your current emotional state" |
| Honest | "This stuff takes time" | "You've got this!" (toxic positivity) |
| Grounded | "Five minutes can shift a lot" | "Transform your life in 30 days" |

## Voice Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use first-person where possible ("I felt that shift when...")
- Keep sentences short — this is mobile, spoken content
- Use everyday language — no jargon, no clinical terms
- Acknowledge struggle before offering solution
- Normalise the experience without minimising it

**Don't:**
- Use overly cheerful language ("Amazing!" "So exciting!")
- Use clinical terms (CBT, dysregulation, psychoeducation)
- Promise outcomes ("Aurie will cure your anxiety")
- Sound like a corporate wellness programme
- Use generic affirmations that feel empty

## Examples

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "We're here to support your mental health journey!" | "It's okay to not have it figured out." |
| "Practice mindfulness today for a better tomorrow!" | "Two minutes of breathing. That's it." |
| "You are worthy of healing!" | "This is hard. You don't have to handle it alone." |
| "Aurie is your 24/7 mental wellness companion!" | "Someone to talk to, whenever you need it." |

## Humour

Gentle, self-aware, never at the expense of the user's struggle. Think "knowing smile" not "punchline."
