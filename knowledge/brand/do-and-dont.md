# Brand Do's and Don'ts

## Content Guardrails

These rules apply to all Scout-generated content — video prompts, captions, hooks, and scripts.

---

## Hard Rules (Never Break)

### Never claim Aurie replaces therapy

Bad: "Aurie is better than therapy"
Bad: "Skip the waiting room — Aurie has you covered"
Good: "Support between sessions — or just when you need someone to talk to"

### No medical claims

Bad: "Clinically proven to reduce anxiety"
Bad: "Treats depression and burnout"
Bad: "Recommended by doctors"
Good: "Helps you feel less alone"
Good: "A space to process what's happening"

### Never minimise struggle

Bad: "Anxiety? Just breathe!"
Bad: "You've got this — mindset is everything!"
Bad: "Stop overthinking — it's that simple"
Good: "Anxiety is exhausting. You don't have to white-knuckle it alone."
Good: "Some days are harder. That's real."

### Never show crisis content

- No depictions of self-harm, suicidal ideation, or acute panic attacks
- No graphic descriptions of trauma
- If content touches heavy topics, always end with the 988 reference or a gentle redirect

### No pop-psychology buzzwords used uncritically

Avoid: "toxic", "gaslighting", "narcissist" (as casual labels)
Context matters — these can be used carefully but not as content hooks

---

## Strong Preferences

### Do lead with the feeling, not the solution

Bad opening: "Aurie helps you manage stress with science-backed tools"
Good opening: "You know that feeling when everything's too much and you don't even know why to start?"

### Do use real-life scenarios

Good: "3am, can't sleep, mind racing — Aurie's there"
Bad: "Use Aurie whenever you experience periods of elevated stress"

### Do acknowledge that progress isn't linear

Good: "Some weeks are better than others. That's not failure."
Avoid: "Build your best self — one day at a time!" (implies linear improvement)

### Do include 988 for heavy topics

Whenever content touches on crisis, grief, self-harm, or suicidal ideation — even obliquely —
include: "If you're in crisis, text or call 988."
