# Competitor Analysis

## Overview

Aurie's competitive landscape includes meditation apps, AI chatbot companions, and therapy
platforms. Understanding their positioning helps Scout generate differentiated content.

---

## Comparison Table

| App | Category | Strengths | Weaknesses | Aurie's edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Calm** | Meditation / sleep | Huge brand, celebrities, sleep stories | Passive — no interaction, not personalised | Aurie talks *with* you, not *at* you |
| **Headspace** | Meditation / mindfulness | Polished UX, evidence-based programs | Structured/rigid — feels like a course | Aurie adapts to how you're feeling today |
| **Woebot** | AI CBT chatbot | Evidence-backed CBT, research pedigree | Feels like a worksheet — robotic, clinical | Aurie is warm and conversational, not scripted |
| **Wysa** | AI mental health support | Low-cost, crisis protocols | Text-heavy, limited personality | Aurie is voice-first and learns your patterns |

---

## Deep Dives

### Calm

- **Positioning:** "The #1 app for sleep and meditation"
- **Content style:** Aspirational, premium, celebrity-driven (LeBron James, Harry Styles)
- **Visual aesthetic:** Serene nature, landscapes, dark blue palette
- **Gap:** No real two-way interaction. You can't tell Calm how you're actually feeling.
- **Opportunity:** UGC content showing *real* people, not aspirational imagery

### Headspace

- **Positioning:** "Meditation and mindfulness made simple"
- **Content style:** Animated, cheerful, educational
- **Visual aesthetic:** Orange-led, flat illustration, very branded
- **Gap:** The "daily practice" model assumes consistency — life isn't consistent
- **Opportunity:** Content that meets people where they are ("I haven't meditated in 3 weeks")

### Woebot

- **Positioning:** "Your mental health ally" (CBT-based AI)
- **Content style:** Minimal online presence, scientific papers
- **Visual aesthetic:** Purple/teal, clean but cold
- **Gap:** Feels like therapy homework. Low warmth. High churn.
- **Opportunity:** Warmth is Aurie's biggest differentiator — show it in every video

### Wysa

- **Positioning:** "Emotionally intelligent AI"
- **Content style:** Soft, friendly, but text-heavy
- **Visual aesthetic:** Teal penguin mascot, friendly but juvenile
- **Gap:** No voice. Limited personality depth. India-focused.
- **Opportunity:** Voice-first is genuinely unique. Lead with it.

---

## Content Differentiation Strategy

When Scout generates content:

1. **Don't position against competitors by name** — focus on the feeling, not the comparison
2. **Lead with the gap the others don't fill** — the moment of needing someone and no one being there
3. **Make Aurie feel like a person, not a product** — competitors feel like tools; Aurie feels like support
4. **Use real scenarios, not wellness aesthetics** — Calm owns nature; Headspace owns colour; Aurie owns authenticity
